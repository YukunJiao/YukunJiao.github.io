Hi! I'm Yukun Jiao, a Master's student in Computational Social Science at Linköping University.

My research examines processes of meaning-making and identity construction using computational text analysis, with a focus on how socially meaningful identities emerge and are coordinated in online communities.

Please see my [CV]({{ "/files/CV.pdf" | relative_url }}) for more details.

By the way, I'm also interested in anime and video games!
